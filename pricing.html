Pricing FAQs

Common questions about coworking pricing, contracts and what’s included at The Village Hub Stellenbosch.

How much does a hot desk cost in Stellenbosch?

At The Village Hub, hot desks start at R60/hour, R150 for a half day, R250 for a full day pass, or R3,000/month for unlimited weekday access. We also offer 5-day (R1,000) and 10-day (R1,600) packages for hybrid workers.

What does a private office cost?

Private offices at The Village Hub: the Village Personal Office (1–2 seater) is R6,950/month, the Village Private Office (3 seater) is R10,450/month, the Village Team Office (4 seater) is R13,950/month, and the Village Executive Office (6 seater) is R20,950/month. All offices are fully furnished with 24/7 secure access included.

How much is boardroom and meeting room hire in Stellenbosch?

The Village Den (4-seater) is from R180/hour, the Village Chamber (10-seater) is R350/hour, and the Village Boardroom (12-seater) is R390/hour. Conference packages are priced on application.

Are there setup fees or long-term contracts?

No. Memberships are month-to-month with no setup fees and no long-term lock-in. Upgrade, downgrade or cancel with reasonable notice. You can also try a day pass before committing.

What’s included in the price?

All plans include fast fibre Wi-Fi, ergonomic seating, complimentary coffee & tea, printing credits, access to community events, and discounted rates at The Village Table restaurant on site.

Do you offer corporate or team discounts?

Yes. Teams of 3+ on private office plans qualify for volume discounts. Corporate clients booking regular boardroom or event hire can access bulk rates. Contact us for a tailored quote.
