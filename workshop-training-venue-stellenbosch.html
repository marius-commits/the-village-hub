Sizing the room and choosing a layout

Start with your headcount and the kind of session you're running, because the same number of people needs very different amounts of space depending on the format:

  • Boardroom / U-shape - best for 6-14 people who need to discuss, decide or workshop together. Ideal for strategy days, interviews, and small training groups.
  • Classroom / theatre - for larger training or seminar groups where people mostly face the front and take notes.
  • Cabaret / pods - small clustered tables for breakout-heavy workshops where groups work then report back.

A good rule of thumb: book for slightly more space than your headcount suggests. A room that's comfortably full energises a session; a room crammed to capacity makes people tired by lunchtime.

The 8-point venue checklist

Before you confirm any venue, get clear answers to these eight questions:

  • WiFi that holds up. Ask whether it handles a room full of people on video and downloads at once.
  • A screen and how you connect. Confirm the screen size, the input, and whether you can test it before delegates arrive.
  • Parking for everyone. Free and on-site is best; ask whether guests park where you do.
  • Catering and coffee. Where does mid-morning coffee come from, and is lunch on-site?
  • Room control. Aircon, lighting, and whether you can rearrange the furniture without a fee.
  • Access times. Can you get in early to set up, and does the hire cover breaks and overruns?
  • Noise and privacy. Is the room properly separated from a busy open-plan area?
  • A clear, all-in price. Room, WiFi, parking, screen - what's included, in writing, before the day.
