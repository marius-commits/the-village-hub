Event Hire FAQs

What kinds of events can I host?

Workshops, training, product launches, networking events, team off-sites, masterclasses, board strategy days and small conferences.

How many people does the event space hold?

From 10 up to 60+ depending on layout (theatre, classroom, banquet, cocktail). Talk to us about specifics.

Is catering available?

Yes. The Village Table restaurant is on site — coffee, breakfast platters, lunch buffets, canapés and full dinner service all available.

Do you offer accommodation for multi-day events?

Yes. The Village Suites are on the estate — ideal for delegates. Group rates available.

Can I host an evening event after coworking hours?

Yes — evening events are accommodated by arrangement, with site rules and an after-hours surcharge where applicable.
