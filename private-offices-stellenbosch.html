Private Office FAQs

How much does a private office cost in Stellenbosch?

The Village Personal Office (1–2 seater) is R6,950/month, the Village Private Office (3 seater) is R10,450/month, the Village Team Office (4 seater) is R13,950/month, and the Village Executive Office (6 seater) is R20,950/month. All furnished with 24/7 secure access and no setup fees.

Are offices furnished?

Yes — desks, ergonomic chairs, storage. Move in with a laptop and you’re productive the same day.

What’s included?

Furniture, fibre Wi-Fi, electricity, cleaning, printing credits, kitchen access, coffee & tea, mail handling, community events, member rates on meeting rooms, 24/7 secure access.

Is there a long-term contract?

No — month-to-month. We can also accommodate longer commitments at preferential rates.

Can my office be branded?

For longer-term tenants — yes. Light branding, custom furniture and additional fit-out can be arranged.
