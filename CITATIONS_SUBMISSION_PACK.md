## Why this works (60-second briefing for anyone helping)

Google's local-pack algorithm relies heavily on three signals: **proximity** (where the searcher is), **relevance** (your category and on-page content) and **prominence** (signals from elsewhere on the web). Citations build prominence. The more high-quality, consistent NAP citations across the web, the more Google trusts that the business is real, established, and locally relevant.

The key word is **consistent**. The NAP at the top of this doc is the canonical version — every submission must use it word-for-word. "1st Floor" vs "First Floor", "+27 72" vs "072", "Stellenbosch" vs "Stellenbosch CBD" — small inconsistencies dilute the signal.

Submit in batches of 3–5 per session to avoid mistakes. Save the confirmation emails in a folder called "VH Citations" — useful when you spot-check whether a listing is still live in 6 months.
