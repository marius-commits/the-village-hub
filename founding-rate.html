Questions

What happens after six months?+

Your membership carries on at the normal rate. We will talk to you before that happens, not surprise you with an invoice. If the Hub is not working for you by then, you can stop.

Do I have to commit to six months?+

The Village Resident and Village Local rates carry a six month commitment, because you are getting half price for exactly that period. Visitor and Wanderer stay month to month.

Is this only for Nooitgedacht residents?+

No. Anyone can take the founding rate. We are telling the village first because they are closest, and because a two minute walk is hard to beat.

What is included in the price?+

Fibre, power at every seat, filter coffee, the kitchen, parking on site, the member app, and member rates on both boardrooms. Printing is charged separately.

I am already a member. Can I move onto this?+

The founding rate is for new members. If you are already with us, speak to Christel. We would rather find you something that works than have you read about a rate you cannot have.

Where exactly are you?+

First floor of The Foundry, Sauvignon Street, Nooitgedacht Village, Stellenbosch. Above The Village Table. Parking at the door.
