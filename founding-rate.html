Questions

What happens after six months?+

Your membership carries on at the normal rate. We will talk to you before that happens rather than surprise you with an invoice, and if the Hub is not working for you by then you can stop.

How do I use the code?+

Choose your membership above, which opens the member portal. Enter VILLAGE50 or VILLAGE30 at checkout, depending on which membership you picked. The discount shows on the invoice before you pay anything.

Is this only for Nooitgedacht residents?+

No, anyone can take it. We are telling the village first because they are closest, and a two minute walk is hard to argue with.

Am I locked in?+

Village Resident is month to month. Village Local, Visitor and Wanderer carry a one month commitment, which is how they were already set up. Nothing here adds a longer term.

I am already a member. Can I move onto this?+

The founding rate is for new members. If you are already with us, speak to Christel rather than sitting on it. We would far rather find you something that works than have you read about a rate you cannot have.

Where exactly are you?+

First floor of The Foundry, Sauvignon Street, Nooitgedacht Village, Stellenbosch. Above The Village Table, with parking at the door. Open in Google Maps

Fifteen memberships, then it closes

The founding rate ends on 31 December 2026 or when the fifteen are taken, whichever comes first.
