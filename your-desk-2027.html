FAQ

Before you sign up.

Do the prices include VAT?

No. R1,500 and R1,750 a month exclude 15% VAT. With VAT that is R1,725 for any desk and R2,012.50 for a dedicated desk.

How does it work out per day?

On 22 working days a month, any desk is about R78 a day and a dedicated desk about R91 a day, both including VAT. Wi-Fi and coffee are included.

Is there a minimum commitment?

None. There is no minimum commitment. You pay month to month from the day you sign up, and the 50% off price holds for up to 12 months. The offer is for new members.

What is the difference between the two desks?

Any desk means you sit at an available desk on the floor each day. A dedicated desk is yours to leave set up. Both are half the normal monthly price: R3,000 for any desk and R3,500 for a dedicated desk.

How many spots are there?

Twelve, and sign-ups close on 31 December 2026.

What happens after 12 months?

We'll confirm renewal pricing before your term ends.

I signed up to Your Desk 2027. What now?

This offer replaces Your Desk 2027. We will contact you directly about your membership.
