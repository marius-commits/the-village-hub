FAQ

Before you lock it in.

Do the prices include VAT?

No. R1,900, R1,805 and R1,710 a month all exclude 15% VAT.

What is the minimum?

Three months, billed from 1 January 2027. The complimentary rest of 2026 is only with that three-month minimum. You can take six months, or commit through to the end of 2027, for a lower monthly price.

Is this a dedicated desk?

No. Your Desk 2027 is an any-desk membership, not a reserved desk. You use an available desk each day. A dedicated desk you can leave set up is the Village Resident plan, at its normal monthly price on the pricing page.

What happens after the term?

We'll confirm renewal pricing before your term ends.

I already have a founding membership.

The founding rate is closed to new sign-ups. If you already have it, you keep your price. Your Desk 2027 is the membership open now.
