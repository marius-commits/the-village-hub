Nooitgedacht Village Stellenbosch: What It Is, Who Lives & Works There

The Village Hub · 24 August 2026 · 6 min read

If you have driven the R304 between Stellenbosch and Klapmuts recently, you have passed a boomed entrance on your left with the words Nooitgedacht Village above it. Most people who see the sign for the first time assume it is a housing estate. It is — but that is only about half the story.

This guide answers the questions we get asked on the phone every week: Where exactly is it? Is it secure? What is inside? Can I work there if I don't live there? If you have found this page because you are considering an office at The Village Hub, moving your family into the estate, or trying to figure out how to get here for a meeting, everything you need is below.

The short version: Nooitgedacht Village is a private, mixed-use lifestyle estate on the R304 outside Stellenbosch. It combines residential homes, a preschool and school, a chapel, retail and restaurants, and The Village Hub — Stellenbosch's premium coworking space and boutique accommodation. It is roughly 8 minutes from Stellenbosch town centre and open to the public through the daytime.

Where Nooitgedacht Village actually is

The estate sits on the R304 (also called Bottelary Road), between Stellenbosch and Klapmuts. From central Stellenbosch it is an 8-minute drive north; from the N1 at Klapmuts it is a 4-minute drive south. The entrance is signposted and boomed, with facial recognition at the visitor gate.

What that means practically: you are ten minutes from Stellenbosch's business district, ten minutes from Paarl via the R44, twenty minutes from Somerset West and Durbanville, and 35 minutes from Cape Town International Airport. That location is the reason a growing number of Stellenbosch professionals commute out of town to work here — the traffic runs the other way, the parking is free, and the R304 corridor is faster than fighting for a bay on Andringa Street.

GPS coordinates: -33.8766828, 18.8213637.

A private estate, but not a closed one

Nooitgedacht Village is a private lifestyle estate managed by its Home Owners Association. There is 24/7 controlled access, security patrols, and a facial recognition system for regular residents and business tenants. Visitors — including anyone attending a meeting at The Village Hub, a coffee at Foundry Coffee Roasters, or dinner at one of the restaurants — clear through the visitor process at the boom, which for daytime commercial visits is quick and painless.

The upshot: you get the calm of an estate — no through-traffic, no street noise, ample free parking — with the accessibility of a working village. That combination is unusual, and it is a large part of why the Hub reads as premium when you walk in.

What is actually inside Nooitgedacht Village

The estate is deliberately mixed-use. In a normal week, the same driveway takes children to preschool, delivers parcels to a home office, welcomes a family for lunch, hosts a corporate boardroom meeting, and lets a guest check into a boutique suite. Here is the honest breakdown.

Homes

Freehold residential homes across a range of typologies. Streets are tree-lined, verges are landscaped, and the estate is walkable end-to-end. Homes come up for resale periodically through local Stellenbosch agents.

Schools and family life

The estate hosts De Kuilen Preschool and Nooitgedacht Village School, meaning families living on-estate have education within walking distance. There is a chapel, open green space, and a rhythm to the village that other work-only precincts (Techno Park, for instance) do not have.

Retail, coffee and F&B

The retail cluster is small, curated and functional rather than sprawling. On any given weekday you can grab a specialty coffee at Foundry Coffee Roasters, pick up cheese and provisions at the Nooitgedacht Dairy Deli, buy bread, and eat a sit-down meal without leaving the estate. It is a working village, not a mall.

The Village Hub — coworking & accommodation

The commercial heart of the precinct is The Village Hub, occupying the first floor of The Foundry building on Sauvignon Street. This is where the estate opens up to non-residents. Inside the Hub you will find:

  • A 36-desk hot-desk floor with fibre, coffee, free secure parking and 24/7 access.
  • Thirteen private offices ranging from two-seater cabins to eight-seater team rooms — see private offices in Stellenbosch.
  • Two boardrooms and a small meeting room — see boardroom hire in Stellenbosch.
  • Boutique accommodation — The Village Stay — for out-of-town guests, board members flying in for a meeting, or teams doing an off-site.

Who Nooitgedacht Village is for

The estate has settled into a clear set of audiences, and it is worth being honest about who thrives here and who does not.

Residents

Families who want school, work, coffee and community inside one boom — but who also want quick access to Stellenbosch, Paarl and the wine routes when the workday ends.

Local business tenants

Professional-services firms, consultancies, tech teams and family businesses that value calm, free secure parking and a premium address that isn't a beige office park. The tenant mix at the Hub currently includes engineering consultancies, ICT firms, professional services and a group specialising in African energy investments.

Stellenbosch commuters who work here

A quiet but growing pattern: people who live in Stellenbosch, Somerset West or Durbanville and drive to Nooitgedacht to work because it beats the fight for parking in town. If that is you, we wrote a separate piece on the coworking option for Somerset West and Durbanville commuters.

Corporates hiring the boardroom

The 12-seater Village Boardroom and Village Chamber are used regularly for offsite strategy days, board meetings, interviews and training sessions by companies who want a serious, professional room without the sterile hotel-conference feel.

Guests staying over

Boutique suites for out-of-towners: family visits, wine-farm weekends, business trips that pair a night on-estate with a meeting in the Hub the next morning. Rates from R1,350/night.

Frequently asked questions

Is Nooitgedacht Village the same as Nooitgedacht Estate?

The names are used loosely. In common speech, "Nooitgedacht Village" refers to the mixed-use residential-and-commercial estate on the R304. The broader Nooitgedacht name is historic and appears on several other properties in the district — check the address before you set the GPS.

Do I need to be a resident to visit?

No. The Village Hub, the coffee shop, the deli and the restaurants are open to the public through normal daytime hours. Access is via the visitor process at the boom.

Is there fibre and reliable power?

Yes. Fibre through the Hub is Herotel-backed and the building runs on backup during load shedding, so a work session doesn't disappear when the grid does. This is one of the practical reasons professionals choose to work here.
