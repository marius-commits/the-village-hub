1. Real Cost Comparison

A 6-person private office in a mid-tier Cape Town business park averages R 14 000 rent + R 3 500 utilities + R 1 200 fibre + R 2 000 furniture lease + cleaning & security. That is ± R 21 000 before coffee, printers or meeting-room build-outs.

At The Village Hub a 6-desk private studio is R 17 400 all-in. You save ± R 3 600 a month and you get enterprise-grade Wi-Fi, daily cleaning, stocked kitchens, bookable boardrooms and a full-time community team.

2. The Hassle Factor

Signing a lease is only step one. Next come insurance quotes, fibre installations, furniture lead-times, key-card programming, generator maintenance, coffee-machine repairs… each item a separate supplier, invoice and potential point of failure.

Coworking transfers every one of those headaches to us. You turn up, open your laptop, and work. If the printer jams, the barista handles it; if the internet drops, our IT supplier is on site within the hour.

3. Flexibility That Matches Real Business Life

Start-ups shrink and grow in weeks, not years. Projects spike and pause. A three-year lease with personal surety makes no sense in that world. The Village Hub lets you add a desk tomorrow, downgrade next quarter, or freeze a membership during quiet months—no lawyers, no penalties. </
