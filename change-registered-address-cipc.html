How to Change Your Registered Address with CIPC

Moving office, leaving a home address, or switching to a virtual office? Here is the CIPC form, the order to do things in, and the five other places your address has to change.

Last updated 24 November 2026

The short answer: a director files form CoR21.1 (Notice of Change of Registered Office) on CIPC's online services, giving the new address and the date it takes effect. It usually costs nothing to file. CIPC does not tell SARS, your bank or Google, so you update those yourself, in the order below.

Before you file: get the new address right

Your registered office is where CIPC, SARS and anyone serving legal papers will send documents. It must be a real physical address in South Africa where someone can receive post for the company. If you are leaving a home address, line up the new one first: a signed lease, or a virtual office agreement that explicitly allows registered-office use.

Have these ready: your company registration number, the director's CIPC customer code and password, the full new address with postal code, and the effective date.

Step by step: filing CoR21.1

  1. Log in to CIPC online services with the customer code of a director or an authorised filer.
  2. Choose the company maintenance option for changing the registered office, and select your company.
  3. Enter the new physical address (and postal address if it differs) and the effective date.
  4. Submit. Keep the reference number and the confirmation CIPC emails you.
  5. Check the company record a few working days later to confirm the new address shows.

If an accountant or company secretary files for you, they will need a director's authority to do it.

How long it takes and what it costs

Filing takes about ten minutes online. Processing is normally a few working days, longer at busy times. CIPC has not charged a filing fee for a change of registered office in recent years, but check the current fee schedule before you file.

Common mistakes

  • Using a PO Box. A registered office needs a physical address.
  • Using an address you have no agreement for. If papers are served there and nobody passes them on, you may miss a deadline.
  • Updating CIPC but not SARS, so tax letters keep going to the old address.
  • Leaving the old address on Google, which splits your reviews and confuses customers.

Frequently asked questions

How do I change my registered address with CIPC?

File a CoR21.1 Notice of Change of Registered Office on the CIPC online services with the new address and the date it takes effect. A director or authorised representative files it, and the change shows on the company's record once CIPC processes it.

Is there a fee to change a registered address with CIPC?

CIPC has not charged a filing fee for a CoR21.1 change of registered address in recent years. Check the current CIPC fee schedule before you file, because fees can change.

Does changing my CIPC address update SARS?

No. CIPC and SARS keep separate records. Update your registered and postal address on SARS eFiling separately, and update your bank, your invoices, your website and your Google Business Profile too.
