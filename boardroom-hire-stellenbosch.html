Boardroom Hire FAQs

How much does it cost to hire a boardroom in Stellenbosch?

The Village Boardroom (12-seater) is R390/hour. The Village Chamber (10-seater) is R350/hour. Half-day, full-day and full conference packages (Village Gathering & Village Assembly) are quoted on application based on duration and catering needs.

What’s the difference between the Village Boardroom and Village Chamber?

The Village Boardroom (12 seats) is our most premium room — designed for board meetings, investor pitches and high-stakes client meetings, with acoustic optimisation and executive presentation tools. The Village Chamber (10 seats) is ideal for team strategy days, workshops and longer working sessions.

What's included in the price?

Premium seating, presentation tools, fibre Wi-Fi, coffee & tea service, water and free on-site parking. Catering on request via The Village Table.

How many people do the rooms seat?

The Village Boardroom seats 12; the Village Chamber seats 10. For smaller meetings of up to 4 people, see our Village Den.

Can I host an after-hours board meeting?

After-hours boardroom hire is possible by arrangement, subject to site rules and a small surcharge.

Do you arrange catering?

Yes. The Village Table restaurant is on site — coffee, breakfast platters, lunch and tea breaks can be added to any booking.
