Meeting Room FAQs

How much does it cost to hire a meeting room in Stellenbosch?

The Village Den (4-seater) is from R180/hour, the Village Chamber (10-seater) is R350/hour, and the Village Boardroom (12-seater) is R390/hour. Conference packages are quoted on application. Members receive discounted rates.

Can I book a meeting room for one hour only?

Yes. Meeting rooms are available on an hourly basis with no minimum stay beyond one hour. Book online or via WhatsApp.

Do you offer video conferencing equipment?

Yes — every room includes a screen with HDMI/USB-C, a high-quality conferencing camera, whiteboards and fast fibre Wi-Fi. Bring a laptop and you’re ready to run a call.

Is parking available?

Yes. Free on-site parking at Nooitgedacht Village, easy access from the R304.

Can you arrange catering?

Yes. The Village Table restaurant is on site — we can arrange coffee, teas, breakfast platters, lunch options or full catering for longer meetings. Just let us know in advance.

Need a meeting room today?

Same-day bookings are usually possible. WhatsApp us with your time and group size and we’ll confirm within minutes.
