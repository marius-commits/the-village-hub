Meeting room hire Stellenbosch FAQs

How much is a meeting room in Stellenbosch?

The Village Chamber is R350/hour for up to 10 seats. The Village Boardroom is R390/hour for up to 12 seats.

Can I book for only one hour?

Yes. Rooms are available by the hour, subject to live availability.

Is there free parking?

Yes. The Village Hub is at Nooitgedacht Village and offers free on-site parking for meeting guests.

Is there a restaurant nearby?

Yes. The Village Table is on site downstairs, useful for coffee, lunch and longer team sessions.

Can non-members book?

Yes. You do not need a coworking membership to hire either meeting room.
