How booking works

By the hour, half day or full day: choose your day above and you see what is free. Give your details and we hold the desk and email your invoice. Paying it confirms the booking, and your Wi-Fi code comes by WhatsApp on the day. An unpaid hold lapses after 24 hours, or 30 minutes before the booking if that is sooner.

Monthly plans: choose a plan and give your details. We set up your membership and email your first invoice, and your access starts when it is paid.

First visit? Park in the main estate lot, walk into The Foundry and come up to the first floor. The front desk is open Monday to Friday, 08:00 to 17:00.

Where we are

The Village Hub is at Nooitgedacht Village, just off the R304 between Stellenbosch and Klapmuts, about 10 minutes from central Stellenbosch. Coming from Somerset West, Durbanville or the farms, you skip town traffic and the parking hunt.

Frequently asked questions

How much does it cost to hire a desk in Stellenbosch?

R60 an hour, R150 for a half day or R250 for a full day, excluding VAT. Monthly plans run from R700 (5 desk days) to R1,750 (a dedicated desk) on the current offer.

Booking online guarantees your seat. Hourly and half-day bookings start at least an hour after you book, and a full day for today can be booked until 12:00. Walk-ins are welcome when desks are free.

Is parking included?

Yes, free and unlimited, on-site, for you and your guests. More in our Stellenbosch parking guide.

Monday to Friday, 08:00 to 17:00. Need more? Ask us about dedicated desks and private offices.
