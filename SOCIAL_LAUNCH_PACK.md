## Cross-promotion suggestions

After each post lands, do this 24 hours later:

1. **Comment on at least 3 other people's posts in the Stellenbosch / startup community.** Don't pitch — engage thoughtfully. Your visibility on theirs returns visibility on yours.
2. **Send the post directly to 2 specific people who would actually care.** ("Hey, you mentioned X last week — wrote this up properly, thought of you.") Direct shares drive 10x the read-rate of broadcast posts.
3. **Pin Post 3 (the tech-hub piece) to your LinkedIn profile** for a week. It's the most attention-grabbing of the three and will pull new followers to your profile.
