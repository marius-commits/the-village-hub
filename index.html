Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about coworking at The Village Hub in Stellenbosch.

Where is The Village Hub coworking space located? ↓

The Village Hub is at Nooitgedacht Village in Stellenbosch, Western Cape — just off the R304. We’re a 10-minute drive from central Stellenbosch, 15 minutes from Somerset West, and roughly 35 minutes from Cape Town CBD. There’s ample on-site parking.

What are the hot desk prices in Stellenbosch? ↓

Hot desks at The Village Hub start at R60 per hour, R150 for a half day, R250 for a full day, or R3,000/month for unlimited weekday access. We also offer 5-day (R1,000) and 10-day (R1,600) packages designed for hybrid workers.

Can I hire a boardroom or meeting room in Stellenbosch? ↓

Yes. We offer three meeting spaces for hire: the Village Boardroom (12-seater, R390/hour), the Village Chamber (10-seater, R350/hour), and the Village Den (4-seater, from R180/hour). All include presentation kit, whiteboards and fast Wi-Fi. Conference packages quoted on application.

Do you offer private offices for small teams? ↓

Yes. We offer 1–2 seater, 3-seater, 4-seater and 6-seater private offices — all fully furnished and move-in ready. Private office members get 24/7 secure access, dedicated Wi-Fi and printing credits.

Is The Village Hub available for events and workshops? ↓

Absolutely. We host workshops, training sessions, evening events and conference packages. The event space accommodates intimate gatherings up to corporate conferences with full A/V setup, catering options via The Village Table, and on-site parking.

What’s included with a Village Hub membership? ↓

All memberships include high-speed fibre Wi-Fi, ergonomic seating, printing credits, complimentary coffee & tea, access to community events, and discounted rates at The Village Table restaurant on site. Private office members get 24/7 secure access.

Can I book a tour or try the space before joining? ↓

Yes. Free tours run during business hours — WhatsApp us to book. You can also buy a day pass from R250 to spend a full working day with us before committing to a plan.
