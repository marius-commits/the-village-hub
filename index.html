FAQ

Good questions.

Anything else? WhatsApp Christel and the team on 072 531 0904.

Where is The Village Hub?

First floor of The Foundry at Nooitgedacht Village, Stellenbosch, just off the R304. Ten minutes from central Stellenbosch, 15 from Somerset West and about 35 from Cape Town CBD, with free on-site parking.

What do hot desks cost?

From R60 an hour, R150 for a half day, R250 for a full day, R1,000 for 5 days, R1,600 for 10 days or R3,000 a month unlimited. A dedicated desk of your own is R3,500 a month. Hub prices exclude VAT. All hot desk plans.

Do you offer a virtual office or business address?

Yes. Village Address (R595), Village Virtual (R1,095) and Village Virtual Pro (R1,695), per month excluding VAT. All three give you a Nooitgedacht Village business address, mail and parcel handling with same-day WhatsApp notification, hot desk days and member boardroom rates. Virtual adds a local phone number and mail forwarding; Pro includes your registered CIPC and SARS address. Three-month minimum, live within 24 hours. Virtual office details.

Is there a student membership?

Yes. The Exam Pass is R795 once-off for unlimited 24/7 study access over the whole November exam season (26 October to 5 December 2026, 20 passes only). Month-to-month student plans run from R350 (Student 5-Day) to R495 (Night Owl, evenings and weekends) and R895 (Student All-Access). Student prices include VAT; bring a valid student card and proof of registration. Student plans.

Can I hire a boardroom or meeting room?

Yes. The Village Boardroom seats 12 at R390 an hour and the Village Chamber seats 10 at R350 an hour. Screens, whiteboards and fast Wi-Fi included; half-day, full-day and conference packages available. Book a room.

Do you have private offices for small teams?

Yes. Furnished 1–2, 3, 4 and 6-seater offices from R6,950 a month, all move-in ready with 24/7 secure access, dedicated Wi-Fi and printing credits. See the offices.

Can I host an event or workshop here?

Yes. Workshops, training days, evening events and conference packages with full A/V, catering from The Village Table and parking for everyone. Event space.

Can I try the space before I commit?

Yes. Free tours run during business hours, just WhatsApp us. Or buy a day pass from R250 and spend a full working day with us first.
