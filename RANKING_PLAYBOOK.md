## 4. Ranking status model

For every tracked query:
- 🔴 20+ or absent: major gap
- 🟠 8–20: page-one opportunity
- 🟡 4–7: striking distance
- 🟢 2–3: top-three presence
- 🏆 1: target achieved

For Maps use geographic-grid top-3 share rather than pretending one universal position exists. For AI search use mention share, citation share and source quality rather than a fictional fixed rank.

## 5. Weekly loop

Every Monday:
1. Pull current data for organic, paid, local, social, CRM and Spacebring where connected.
2. Compare every priority query against prior periods and the previous decision ledger.
3. Identify the single biggest blocker for each high-priority query not at target.
4. Choose the highest-impact intervention across the whole portfolio.
5. Execute pre-approved actions automatically where safe.
6. Verify the change live.
7. Record the intervention, hypothesis, expected effect and evaluation date.
8. Send Marius a decision-first report with at most three owner-only actions.

Do not force a new article every week. The weekly action can be a landing-page improvement, internal-link change, title/meta CTR test, schema fix, technical fix, backlink/citation acquisition, review programme, GBP improvement, directory correction, evidence asset, content refresh or ad optimisation.

## 6. Blocker taxonomy

When a query is not at target, diagnose before acting:
- Relevance gap → improve intent match, title/H1, content and internal links.
- CTR gap → improve title/snippet/offer and search-result promise.
- Authority gap → earn relevant backlinks, mentions and citations.
- Local prominence gap → reviews, GBP completeness, citations, local links.
- Technical gap → crawl/index/canonical/sitemap/performance/schema.
- Cannibalisation → consolidate or differentiate competing pages.
- Low-demand query → deprioritise rather than manufacture content.
- Conversion gap → improve landing page, proof, CTA and speed-to-lead.
- Paid visibility gap → diagnose impression share, Ad Rank, query coverage and quality before changing bids.
- AI citation gap → improve factual extractability, evidence, freshness, entity consistency and third-party corroboration.
